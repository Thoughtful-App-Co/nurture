## 🎯 Key Takeaways

### Critical Finding
Phones track calls/texts but NOT physical presence. This causes Dunbar calculations to be **wrong by 3-5 layers** for roommates, coworkers, and regular activity groups.

### Solution
Two-tier system:
1. Contact-level patterns ("I live with this person")
2. Group activities ("Soccer every Tuesday with 10 people")

### Priority
**P0 - Critical for MVP**. Without this, Nurture's core promise is broken.

### Timeline
**4-6 weeks** for full implementation (8 phases)

### Success Metric
Dunbar accuracy improves from **70% → 85%** (user-reported)
